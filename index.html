Frequently asked

Useful answers before you request.

If your question is specific, WhatsApp is the fastest way to confirm whether the service is suitable.

Do you serve customers outside Kenya?

Yes. Websites, documents, design, data, career and education support can usually be delivered remotely. Government-related assistance is limited to lawful guidance for eligible processes.

How is pricing calculated?

Pricing depends on scope, complexity, urgency and the number of deliverables. A quotation is confirmed after requirements review.

Can you guarantee approval for an application?

No. RINOZ01SKYMARK provides administrative guidance and document readiness support but cannot guarantee decisions by government bodies, employers, universities, insurers or other third parties.

Do you complete academic work on behalf of students?

No. Student support is ethical and focuses on coaching, formatting, research organisation, editing and analysis assistance. Customers remain responsible for their own academic work and institutional compliance.

How are uploaded documents handled?

Documents are stored in protected Netlify object storage and are linked to the signed-in customer's request. Only authorised service operations should access them.

What happens after I submit a request?

The request begins as Pending. The team reviews it, confirms requirements and updates the status as work progresses.
